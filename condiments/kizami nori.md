##  Description

Kizami nori is finely shredded roasted seaweed (nori) that serves as a versatile garnish and flavor enhancer. Unlike full nori sheets used for sushi, these delicate strips are designed to be sprinkled, scattered, and distributed evenly across dishes.

Dr. Umami classifies it as:

> "The micro-dosing of ocean flavor. Each tiny strip contains concentrated marine umami that dissolves on the tongue, releasing waves of briny complexity. It's not just garnish—it's flavor architecture."

## 🧠 Dr. Umami Notes

> "The roasting process is critical—it develops Maillard reaction compounds that add depth beyond raw seaweed. The shredding increases surface area, allowing for even distribution and rapid flavor release. This is not lazy garnishing; this is precision flavor engineering."

**Pro Tip:** Store in an airtight container to maintain crispness. Moisture is the enemy of kizami nori. Sprinkle generously—the ocean is vast, and so should be your generosity.

---

## 🧊 Storage

- Store in a cool, dry place
- Keep away from moisture (critical!)
- Use within 3-6 months of opening for best crispness
- The red cap is not just aesthetic—it's a moisture barrier
