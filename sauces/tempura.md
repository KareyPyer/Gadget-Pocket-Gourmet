## 🧪 Description

This concentrated tempura dipping sauce (tentsuyu) is the traditional accompaniment to light, crispy tempura. It combines dashi, soy sauce, and mirin in precise proportions designed to complement—not compete with—delicate fried seafood and vegetables.

Dr. Umami classifies it as:

> "The yin to tempura's yang. Where tempura is crispy and oily, this sauce is liquid and cleansing. Where tempura is hot, this sauce is room temperature. It's culinary balance personified."

## 🧠 Dr. Umami Notes

> "The concentrated nature of this sauce is intentional. Traditional tempura houses serve it undiluted in small cups, allowing the diner to control the intensity. The dashi-forward profile cleanses the palate between bites of fried goodness. It's not just sauce—it's digestive assistance."

**Pro Tip:** Serve at room temperature in small dipping cups. Add grated daikon radish and ginger for the authentic experience. The daikon's enzymes actually help break down the oil.
