## 🧪 Description

This specialized soy sauce is engineered (in the best way) to complement plain steamed rice. With added sweetness and reduced salt, it creates a caramelized glaze when drizzled over hot rice.

Dr. Umami classifies it as:

> "The Japanese equivalent of putting butter and maple syrup on pancakes, except it's umami instead of sugar. And it's brilliant."

---

## 🍜 Traditional Uses

- **Drizzled over plain steamed rice** (primary use)
- **Rice bowls** (donburi base)
- **Onigiri seasoning**
- **Breakfast rice service**
- **Glaze for grilled rice balls** (yaki onigiri)
- **Fried rice seasoning**
- **Rice porridge** (okayu) enhancement

## 🧠 Dr. Umami Notes

> "The sugar content is approximately 2-3x higher than regular soy sauce, while sodium is reduced by about 30%. This creates a sauce that caramelizes beautifully on hot rice, creating tiny flavor bombs with each grain. It's not dessert, but it's not quite savory either—it's breakfast nirvana."

**Pro Tip:** Drizzle over freshly steamed, piping hot rice. The heat activates the caramelization. Add a raw egg and nori for the ultimate Japanese breakfast.

---

## 🧊 Storage

- Store at room temperature
- Refrigeration not required but extends freshness
- Best used within 6 months of opening
- The sugar content acts as a natural preservative
