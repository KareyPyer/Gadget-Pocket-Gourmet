## 🧪 Description

Kikkoman's Teriyaki Marinade is the gateway drug to Japanese home cooking. This versatile sauce combines traditionally brewed soy sauce with wine, sugar, and aromatic spices to create a glaze that caramelizes beautifully under heat.

Dr. Umami classifies it as:

> "A molecular ballet of Maillard reactions waiting to happen. Apply heat, observe the transformation, and prepare for umami euphoria."

##  Traditional Uses

## 🧠 Dr. Umami Notes

> "The addition of wine distinguishes this from traditional teriyaki. It adds complexity and aids in tenderizing proteins. For optimal results, marinate for 30 minutes minimum, then reduce the marinade to a glaze. Never waste a drop."

**Pro Tip:** Brush onto proteins during the last 5 minutes of grilling to prevent burning the sugars. Patience yields lacquered perfection.
