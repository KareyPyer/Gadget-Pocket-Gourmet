##  Description

Soba Tsuyu is the traditional dipping sauce for cold buckwheat noodles. This concentrated version combines fish extract (dashi), soy sauce, and mirin in precise proportions that honor centuries of Japanese culinary tradition.

Dr. Umami classifies it as:

> "A masterclass in restraint. Unlike its Western cousins, this sauce doesn't shout—it whispers umami secrets to those who know how to listen."

---

## 🍜 Traditional Uses

- **Zaru soba** (cold soba noodles)
- **Mori soba** (chilled buckwheat noodles)
- **Hot soba broth** (diluted with noodle cooking water)
- **Dipping sauce for tempura**
- **Light soup base for udon**

## 🧠 Dr. Umami Notes

> "The dilution ratio is sacred: 1:3 for cold soba dipping, 1:5 for hot broth. The tsuyu must be served with accompaniments—wasabi and chopped scallions are not optional, they are essential. This is ritual, not suggestion."

**Pro Tip:** For authentic zaru soba, serve the tsuyu in small cups with a side of wasabi and finely chopped green onions. Dip noodles gently, don't drown them.
