## 🧪 Description

This premium Tamari represents Kikkoman's commitment to traditional brewing methods. Aged longer than standard tamari, it develops an even deeper color and more concentrated flavor profile.

Dr. Umami classifies it as:

> "The difference between instant coffee and single-origin pour-over. Both will wake you up, but only one will make you weep with joy."

## 🧠 Dr. Umami Notes

> "The traditional brewing process involves natural fermentation in wooden vats, allowing wild yeasts and bacteria to contribute to flavor development. This creates esters and phenols absent in快速-brewed versions. Translation: it tastes better because science says so."

**Pro Tip:** This tamari is thicker and more viscous. Use it sparingly—a little goes a long way. Perfect for drizzling rather than drowning.

---

## 🧊 Storage

- Store upright in a cool, dark place
- Refrigeration recommended after opening
- Best consumed within 12 months of opening
- Protect from light and heat (enemies of flavor)
