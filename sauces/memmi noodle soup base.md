## 🧪 Description

Memmi is Kikkoman's answer to the instant gratification of authentic Japanese noodle soup. This concentrated base combines naturally brewed soy sauce with bonito extract, creating an instant broth that respects tradition while embracing convenience.

Dr. Umami classifies it as:

> "The alchemical distillation of a thousand-year-old culinary tradition into a single bottle. Simply add water and witness the magic."
