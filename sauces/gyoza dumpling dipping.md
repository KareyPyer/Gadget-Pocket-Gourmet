##  Description

Kikkoman's take on dumpling sauce brings the company's legendary brewing expertise to the humble gyoza accompaniment. This sauce combines soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and aromatics in proportions optimized through centuries of flavor science.

Dr. Umami classifies it as:

> "What happens when a Fortune 500 company decides to perfect dumpling sauce. It's not artisanal, but it is engineered for maximum deliciousness. Sometimes corporate R&D serves the people."
