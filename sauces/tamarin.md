##  Description

Kikkoman's Tamari is a wheat-free soy sauce that represents the original form of Japanese soy sauce. Made primarily from soybeans with little to no wheat, it offers a richer, more complex flavor profile than standard shoyu.

Dr. Umami classifies it as:

> "The aristocrat of soy sauces—gluten-free by tradition, not by trend. Its deeper color and mellower taste are the result of centuries-old brewing wisdom."

## 🧠 Dr. Umami Notes

> "Tamari contains approximately 90% soybeans and 10% wheat (or none at all), compared to regular shoyu's 50/50 split. This higher soybean concentration creates a thicker, more viscous sauce with deeper umami. It's not just for celiacs—it's for connoisseurs."

**Pro Tip:** Use tamari when you want a more pronounced soy flavor without overwhelming saltiness. Perfect for delicate dishes where regular soy sauce would dominate.
