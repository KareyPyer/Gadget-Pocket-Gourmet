## 🧪 Description

Haizakura represents the antithesis of mass-produced soy sauce. Crafted by Daikou Shoyu, a traditional Japanese brewery, this dark soy sauce undergoes a two-year aging process in Japanese cedar barrels, developing layers of complexity that industrial methods cannot replicate.

Dr. Umami classifies it as:

> "The difference between fast food and slow food, distilled into a single bottle. Each drop contains 730 days of microbial alchemy and human patience."

##  Dr. Umami Notes

> "The cedar barrel aging imparts subtle woody notes absent in stainless steel-aged sauces. This is not your everyday soy sauce—reserve it for applications where its complexity can shine. Using this on scrambled eggs is culinary sacrilege."

**Pro Tip:** Serve at room temperature to appreciate its full aromatic profile. The paper-wrapped bottle is not just aesthetic—it protects from light degradation. Treat it like fine wine.

---

##  Storage

- Store in a cool, dark place
- Keep away from direct sunlight (hence the paper wrapping)
- Does not require refrigeration but benefits from it
- Indefinite shelf life, but best within 2 years of opening
